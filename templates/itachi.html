THE MAN BEHIND THE EYES

A STORY WRITTEN IN SHADOWS

Itachi's character is closely connected to the Uchiha clan and to his relationship with his younger brother Sasuke.

His journey places him between the interests of his clan, his village and his family.

Rather than presenting his story as a simple battle between good and evil, the Itachi storyline explores choices, consequences and conflicting loyalties.
