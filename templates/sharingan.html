DŌJUTSU

SHARINGAN

THE EYE OF PERCEPTION

The Sharingan is a dōjutsu associated with the Uchiha clan. It allows its users to perceive movements, copy certain techniques and see chakra in ways ordinary shinobi cannot.

Its abilities develop through different stages, with the Mangekyō Sharingan representing an advanced evolution of the dōjutsu.
