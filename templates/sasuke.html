SASUKE UCHIHA

THE LAST SURVIVOR OF A BROKEN CLAN

Sasuke Uchiha is the younger brother of Itachi Uchiha and one of the central characters of Naruto. After surviving the destruction of the Uchiha clan, Sasuke dedicated himself to becoming powerful enough to confront his brother.

His journey moves from revenge and hatred toward understanding, identity and his place in the shinobi world.
